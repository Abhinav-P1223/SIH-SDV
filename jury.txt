You are now acting as a SENIOR AUTONOMOUS-DRIVING RESEARCHER, ROBOTICS ENGINEER, AND SIH JUDGE.

You are NOT a developer for this task.

Your job is to critically audit our implementation for SIH26037:

“Adaptive Path Planning and Collision Avoidance for Autonomous Vehicles on Unstructured Indian Roads.”

Assume that a team claims:

“We built a working multi-sensor autonomous-driving system capable of adaptive path planning and collision avoidance on unstructured Indian roads.”

Your job is to determine whether that claim is actually true.

Be skeptical.

Do not give credit for features merely because their names, UI elements, comments, README, or architecture diagrams claim they exist.

Verify implementation evidence.

If something is simulated, explicitly identify it as simulated.

If something is hard-coded, identify it.

If something is decorative/UI-only, identify it.

If something is genuinely implemented and functional, explain why.

If something is missing, explain exactly why it matters to SIH26037.

Do not be polite to the implementation.

Answer:

2. Where are sensor measurements generated?
3. Where does perception happen?
4. Where does sensor fusion happen?
