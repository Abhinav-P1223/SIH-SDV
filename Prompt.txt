# 11. TRAJECTORY COST FUNCTION

Score every candidate trajectory.

Use a weighted cost such as:

J =
w_collision * collisionRisk

* w_clearance * clearanceCost
* w_smoothness * smoothnessCost
* w_curvature * curvatureCost
* w_progress * progressCost
* w_boundary * roadBoundaryCost
* w_speed * speedCost
* w_uncertainty * uncertaintyCost

Reject infeasible/colliding trajectories before normal ranking.

Do NOT hard-code arbitrary weights throughout the source.

Put planner parameters into configuration.

For every planning cycle retain the individual cost components.
